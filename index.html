Our Heritage

A Taste Born
From the Rift Valley

LakeBite was born in 2013 from a simple vision: to honour Kenya's extraordinary culinary diversity in a space as beautiful as the landscapes that inspire it. Nestled in the heart of Nairobi's Westlands, we bring together the rich traditions of the Rift Valley, the spiced heritage of the Swahili coast, and the bold innovation of modern African cuisine.

Every ingredient is sourced with intention — from the slopes of Mount Kenya to the shores of Lake Victoria — ensuring that every plate tells the story of this remarkable land.
